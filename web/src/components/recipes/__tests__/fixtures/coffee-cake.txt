# Cinnamon Streusel Coffee Cake

Servings: 9\
Prep Time: 15 minutes\
Cook Time: 40 minutes

## Ingredients

### Cake

- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 1/2 cup sour cream
- 1/4 cup milk
- 2 tsp vanilla extract

### Cinnamon Ribbon

- 1/3 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon

### Streusel Topping

- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup brown sugar, packed
- 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted
- 1 pinch salt

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease or line an 8×8-inch baking pan.
2. Make streusel by mixing flour, brown sugar, cinnamon, salt, and melted butter until crumbly. Set aside.
3. Mix brown sugar and cinnamon for the ribbon in a separate bowl.
4. Whisk melted butter and granulated sugar. Add eggs, sour cream, milk, and vanilla.
5. Stir in flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt until just combined.
6. Spread half the batter into the pan. Sprinkle with cinnamon ribbon, then spread remaining batter on top.
7. Sprinkle streusel evenly over the batter.
8. Bake 35–45 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs. Cool 20 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Sour cream produces a richer, more tender cake than Greek yogurt.
- Avoid overmixing the batter.
- Coffee cake does not contain coffee; it is traditionally served alongside it.
- Store airtight at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze individual portions.
