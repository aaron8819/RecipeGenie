Teriyaki Chicken and Broccoli Rice Bowls

Category: Chicken
Servings: 4
Prep time: 15 minutes
Cook time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

Chicken

* 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
* 1 tsp avocado oil

Teriyaki Sauce &amp; Marinade

* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
* ⅓ cup water
* ⅓ cup soy sauce
* 3 tbsp brown sugar
* 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
* 1½ tbsp mirin
* 1 tbsp cornstarch
* 1 tbsp cold water

Rice

* 1 cup dry jasmine rice
* Water

Broccoli

* 4 cups broccoli florets
* 2 tsp avocado oil
* 3 tbsp water
* Salt and black pepper, to taste

Optional Garnish

* Green onions, sliced
* Sesame seeds

Instructions

1. Marinate chicken: Mix garlic, ginger, ⅓ cup water, soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, and mirin. Reserve ⅔ for sauce. Marinate chicken in remaining ⅓ for at least 30 minutes in the refrigerator.
2. Cook rice: Rinse jasmine rice and cook in rice cooker according to instructions.
3. Air fry chicken: Preheat air fryer to 380°F. Remove chicken from marinade, discard used marinade, and lightly coat with avocado oil. Air fry 14–18 minutes, flipping halfway, until internal temperature reaches at least 165°F. Rest 3–5 minutes.
4. Cook broccoli: Heat avocado oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Sear broccoli 2–3 minutes, add 3 tbsp water, cover, and steam 2 minutes. Uncover and cook until excess water evaporates. Season lightly.
5. Make sauce: Simmer reserved teriyaki sauce for 2–3 minutes. Mix cornstarch with 1 tbsp cold water, stir into sauce, and cook 1–2 minutes until thickened.
6. Assemble: Slice chicken and toss with ⅔ of the sauce. Serve over rice with broccoli, drizzle with remaining sauce, and add optional garnishes.

Notes

* Marinating: Refrigerate chicken in marinade for at least 30 minutes, ideally 1–2 hours.
* Extra caramelization: After glazing, air fry chicken at 400°F for 1–2 minutes.
* Chicken texture: Thighs are safe at 165°F but often more tender at 175–185°F.
* Air fryer: Cook in batches if necessary to avoid overcrowding.
* Sauce consistency: Add water 1 tbsp at a time if too thick.
* Higher protein: Increase chicken to 2 lb for larger portions.
* Meal prep: Refrigerate leftovers for up to 3–4 days. Store extra sauce separately.
