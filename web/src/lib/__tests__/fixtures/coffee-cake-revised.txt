# Cinnamon Streusel Sour Cream Coffee Cake

Servings: 9\
Prep Time: 20 minutes\
Cook Time: 40 minutes

## Ingredients

### Cinnamon Streusel

- 2/3 cup packed brown sugar
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter, cold and cubed

### Cake

- 1 1/3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup full-fat sour cream
- 2 tbsp milk

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease or line an 8×8-inch baking pan with parchment paper.
2. **Make streusel:** Mix brown sugar, flour, and cinnamon. Work in cold butter with a fork or your fingers until coarse crumbs form. Refrigerate.
3. **Mix dry ingredients:** Whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
4. **Cream butter and sugar:** Beat softened butter and sugar with a stand mixer on medium-high speed for about 3 minutes until creamy.
5. **Add wet ingredients:** Mix in eggs and vanilla, then sour cream. Scrape down the bowl as needed.
6. **Finish batter:** Mix in dry ingredients on low speed until just combined. Add milk and mix briefly. Batter will be thick.
7. **Layer:** Spread half the batter in the pan. Sprinkle with half the streusel. Gently spread remaining batter on top, then add remaining streusel.
8. **Bake:** Bake 35–40 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out without wet batter. Cool in pan for 15 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Based on Sally's Baking Addiction Sour Cream Coffee Cake with Crumb Topping.
- Source: [https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/coffee-cake-recipe/](https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/coffee-cake-recipe/)
- Use an 8×8-inch pan, not 9×9.
- Bring eggs, sour cream, and milk to room temperature before mixing.
- Keep streusel butter cold and refrigerate streusel until layering.
- Avoid overmixing once flour is added.
- The top batter layer doesn't need to be perfectly smooth.
- A stand mixer with paddle attachment is recommended for creaming butter and sugar.
- Store airtight at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze individual slices.
