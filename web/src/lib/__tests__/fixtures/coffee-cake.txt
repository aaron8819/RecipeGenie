# Cinnamon Streusel Sour Cream Coffee Cake

Servings: 9
Prep Time: 20 minutes
Cook Time: 40 minutes

## Ingredients

### Cinnamon Streusel
- 2/3 cup packed brown sugar (135 g)
- 3/4 cup all-purpose flour (95 g)
- 2 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter, cold and cubed (85 g)

### Cake
- 1 1/3 cups all-purpose flour (166 g)
- 1 tsp baking powder
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened (113 g)
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar (150 g)
- 2 large eggs, room temperature
- 2 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 cup full-fat sour cream, room temperature (120 g)
- 2 tbsp milk (30 ml)

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Grease or line an 8×8-inch baking pan with parchment paper.
2. Make streusel: Combine brown sugar, flour, and cinnamon. Work in cold butter with a fork or your fingers until coarse crumbs form. Refrigerate.
3. Whisk cake flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
4. Using a stand mixer with paddle attachment, beat softened butter and sugar on medium-high speed for about 3 minutes until creamy.
5. Mix in eggs and vanilla, then sour cream. Scrape down the bowl as needed.
6. Mix in dry ingredients on low speed until just combined. Add milk and mix briefly. Batter will be thick.
7. Spread half the batter in the pan. Sprinkle with about half the streusel. Gently spread remaining batter on top, then add remaining streusel.
8. Bake 35–40 minutes until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out without wet batter. Cool in pan for 15 minutes before slicing.

## Notes

- Based on Sally's Baking Addiction Sour Cream Coffee Cake with Crumb Topping.
- Source: https://sallysbakingaddiction.com/coffee-cake-recipe/
- Use an 8×8-inch pan, not 9×9.
- Use room-temperature cake ingredients and cold butter for streusel.
- Keep streusel cold until layering so the crumbs hold their shape.
- Don't overmix after adding flour.
- The top batter layer doesn't need to be perfectly smooth.
- A stand mixer is recommended for creaming the butter and sugar.
- Optional: Drizzle with vanilla icing after baking.
- Store airtight at room temperature for up to 3 days or freeze individual slices.
