Teriyaki Chicken and Broccoli Rice Bowls

Category: Chicken
Servings: 4
Prep time: 15 minutes
Marinate time: 30 minutes
Cook time: 25 minutes

Ingredients

Chicken

* 1½ lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
* 1 tsp avocado oil

Teriyaki Sauce &amp; Marinade

* 4 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
* ⅓ cup water
* ⅓ cup low-sodium soy sauce
* 3 tbsp brown sugar
* 1½ tbsp rice vinegar
* 1½ tbsp mirin
* 1 tbsp cornstarch
* 1 tbsp cold water (for cornstarch slurry)

Rice

* 1 cup dry jasmine rice
* 1¼ cups water (or as directed on package)

Broccoli

* 4 cups broccoli florets
* 2 tsp avocado oil
* 3 tbsp water
* Salt and black pepper, to taste

Optional Garnish

* Sliced green onions
* Sesame seeds

Instructions

1. Prepare the Teriyaki Marinade

Combine garlic, ginger, ⅓ cup water, soy sauce, brown sugar, rice vinegar, and mirin in a bowl. Stir until sugar dissolves.

Reserve ⅔ of the mixture in a separate container for the finishing sauce.

Place chicken thighs in a bowl or resealable bag with the remaining ⅓ of the marinade. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes, ideally 1–2 hours.

2. Cook the Rice

Rinse jasmine rice until water runs mostly clear.

Combine rice and 1¼ cups water in a saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce heat to low, and simmer 12–15 minutes (or follow package instructions).

Remove from heat and let rest, covered, for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork.

3. Air Fry the Chicken

Preheat air fryer to 380°F for 3 minutes.

Remove chicken from marinade and let excess liquid drip off. Discard used marinade.

Lightly brush chicken with 1 tsp avocado oil.

Arrange thighs in a single layer without overcrowding. Cook in batches if necessary.

Air fry at 380°F for 14–18 minutes, flipping halfway through. Cook until the thickest part reaches at least 165°F. For more tender thighs, aim for approximately 175–185°F.

Rest chicken for 3–5 minutes before slicing.

4. Cook the Broccoli

Heat 2 tsp avocado oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.

Add broccoli florets in a single layer. Sear for 2–3 minutes, stirring minimally to develop browned edges.

Add 3 tbsp water and immediately cover. Steam for approximately 2 minutes until tender-crisp.

Remove lid and cook another 30–60 seconds to evaporate excess water.

Season lightly with salt and black pepper.

5. Thicken the Teriyaki Sauce

While chicken cooks, pour the reserved, unused teriyaki sauce into a small saucepan.

Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat for 2–3 minutes.

Mix 1 tbsp cornstarch with 1 tbsp cold water until smooth.

Slowly stir slurry into simmering sauce. Cook for 1–2 minutes until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.

If sauce becomes too thick, add water 1 tbsp at a time.

6. Glaze the Chicken

Slice rested chicken into strips or bite-sized pieces.

Toss chicken with approximately ⅔ of the thickened teriyaki sauce until evenly coated.

Optional: Return glazed chicken to the air fryer at 400°F for 1–2 minutes for caramelized edges. Watch carefully to prevent burning.

7. Assemble Bowls

Divide jasmine rice evenly among 4 bowls.

Top each with teriyaki chicken and approximately 1 cup broccoli.

Drizzle remaining teriyaki sauce over the bowls.

Garnish with sliced green onions and sesame seeds, if desired.

Notes

* For higher protein portions, increase chicken thighs to 2 lb.
* Cook chicken in batches for the best browning.
* Keep broccoli slightly crisp to contrast with the tender chicken.
* Refrigerate leftovers in airtight containers for up to 3–4 days.
* For meal prep, store extra sauce separately to prevent the rice from becoming soggy.
